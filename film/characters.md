# Character lock sheets

Generate each character as a still FIRST, approve the face, then use that
still as `start_image` / `image_references` on every shot that character
appears in. This is the only reliable way to stop the face changing between
shots. Do not animate a character before its lock still is approved.

Model: `soul_2` (portrait/editorial) or `gpt_image_2_5`. Aspect 16:9 for
plates that will drive 16:9 shots; generate a 9:16 variant for vertical.

**Everyone below is a fictional model, not an Apex client.** Nothing in the
film may state or imply that these people are customers.
