# Music and sound-design brief

**Higgsfield cannot produce this.** Its audio tool generates speech only and
explicitly declines general music and sound-effect requests. This brief is
written to be handed to a music generation tool, a library licence, or a
composer.

## The track

Original instrumental hip-hop. No imitation of any named artist.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Tempo | 88-96 BPM |
| Length | 3:05 (5s of tail past the last card) |
| Key feel | Minor, dark, cinematic |
| Mix target | -14 LUFS integrated, true peak -1 dBTP |

Layers: deep sub bass, hard kick, crisp snare with room, layered percussion,
cinematic impacts on section changes, atmospheric pads, dark synth, subtle
low strings, occasional brass stab. Expensive and aggressive without becoming
chaotic — space matters more than density.

## Section map — must line up with the edit

| Time | Section | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:22 | Tension | Atmosphere only. Sub pulse every 2 bars. No drums. Rising noise floor. |
| 0:22-0:30 | Pre-drop | Percussion enters. Riser. Hard stop at 0:29. |
| 0:30-1:20 | DROP | Full beat. Hardest kick in the film. This is the production montage. |
| 1:20-1:55 | Sustained | Beat continues, drums thinned. Melodic element takes over. |
| 1:55-2:12 | Breath | Drums drop to kick and hat. Space. This is the before/after turn. |
| 2:12-2:25 | Rebuild | Percussion stacks back. Riser into Act V. |
| 2:25-2:48 | BIGGEST HIT | Everything. Brass stabs on the section titles. |
| 2:48-2:56 | Strip | Down to sub and one pad. The close. |
| 2:56-3:05 | Card | Single sustained low note under the final card. Fade. |

## Sound design — separate stems

Cut against picture, not baked into the music:

camera shutter · lens click · camera power-on and record beep · studio room
tone · footsteps on concrete · light switch and ballast hum · strobe fire ·
keyboard and jog wheel · timeline scrub · slider and gimbal movement ·
restaurant kitchen ambience · city night ambience · bass impacts on cuts ·
whooshes on whip pans · cloth and wardrobe movement · light crowd murmur for
the corporate shot

Rule: the shutter at 0:02 and the strobe at 1:22 (shot 30) are the two
loudest single events before the drop. They are the punctuation.

## What must never appear

No voiceover claiming results. No testimonial read. No invented client name.
No performance claim. The film sells capability, not outcomes.
