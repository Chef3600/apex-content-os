# Portfolio image QC sheet

Score **every** image before it enters `site/images/portfolio/`.
This is the website-image rubric. The production rubric in
`docs/fidelity-gate.md` still governs client work.

| Dimension | Max | What you are looking at |
|---|---:|---|
| Visual quality | 15 | Would this sit on a premium brand's site? |
| Reference fidelity | 20 | Does the subject match the brief's shape, color, material? For spec work with no client reference, does it match the prompt's stated subject exactly? |
| Realism | 15 | Materials, texture, food behavior. Anything reading as plastic or painted? |
| Composition | 10 | Deliberate framing, or accidental? |
| Lighting | 10 | Did it follow the stated key direction, contrast and falloff? |
| Camera | 10 | Lens character, perspective, focus plane as directed? |
| Commercial usefulness | 10 | Would a paying client place this in a campaign? |
| Brand fit | 10 | Bright, premium, editorial, colorful. Does it belong next to the other twelve? |

**90+ approved · 80–89 revise · 70–79 regenerate · below 70 reject.**

## Automatic rejects — no score can save these

- Visible text, logos or packaging copy of any kind
- A recognisable real brand's product identity
- A person whose face implies endorsement
- Impossible food or liquid physics
- Wrong anatomy (shrimp tail segments, hands, fingers)
- Reads as AI art rather than commercial photography
- Dark and murky when the brief called for bright

## Three-attempt rule

Max three attempts per shot. On the third failure, **stop.** Diagnose the
cause and redesign the shot or drop it. Do not keep spending credits.
