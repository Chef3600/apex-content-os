# Scope and terms

Plain-language terms that go with the Pilot. They exist so that the first
disagreement is settled by a document written before the money, not by
whoever argues harder afterwards.

**Not legal advice.** This is an operating document. Before it is used as the
actual contract on a job above roughly $5,000, on any job with a corporate
client, or anywhere it would be the only written agreement, have a Nevada
attorney read it once. That is a one-time cost and it is cheap.

## The engagement

Apex Content Studio is a trade name of **Apex Hospitality Group LLC**. The
LLC is the contracting party on every invoice and every agreement.

## Included in the Pilot - $1,500

| | |
|---|---|
| Subject | **One** product or dish, named in the proposal |
| Finished assets | 10 |
| Formats | 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 of each - 30 delivered files |
| Revisions | One round |
| Rights | Perpetual, worldwide, all channels, no re-licensing fee |
| Creative plan | Delivered before the balance is due |
| Turnaround | 7 business days from product in hand and deposit cleared |

## What a revision is

**A revision changes an asset. It does not add a concept.**

Colour, crop, retouching, background cleanup, text placement, and swapping
which frame is the hero: revision. A new dish, a new angle that was not in
the approved creative plan, a second product, a different format set: new
scope, quoted separately.

This one definition prevents most of the margin loss in this business. Say it
on the call, before the deposit, in these words.

## Not included

Media buying, ad account management, offer or pricing strategy, landing pages,
copywriting beyond asset-level text, talent, location fees, permits, and
travel outside the Las Vegas metro. Any of it can be quoted; none of it is
assumed.

## Never included, at any price

- Performance guarantees. Apex sells production capacity, not results.
- Any product, ingredient, credential, or claim that is not real.
- Fabricated testimonials, reviews, or customers.

## Payment

- **50% to book.** Production starts when it clears and the product is in hand.
- **50% on delivery**, net 7.
- Deposits are non-refundable once production begins, because the calendar
  slot and the prep are consumed whether or not the client continues.
- If the client's product or approvals stall the job more than 14 days, the
  balance becomes due and the remaining work is rescheduled.

## Rights and portfolio

The client owns perpetual commercial use of the delivered assets on delivery
of final payment.

Apex retains the right to show the work **only with written permission.**
Ask for it at delivery, in writing, and record how it was given:

```
node tools/job.mjs rights <job> yes "emailed 'yes, go ahead' 2026-10-02"
```

No written yes means the work stays off the site and out of every pitch. A
verbal "sure, no problem" is not a record.

## Cancellation

Either side can end the engagement in writing. The client pays for work
completed to that point, at the proportion of assets delivered. Apex refunds
anything collected above that within 14 days.

## Confidentiality

Unreleased products, recipes, pricing, and launch dates stay confidential
until the client publishes them. This one matters more than it looks - in a
small food market it is the entire reputation.
