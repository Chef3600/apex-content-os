# Outreach rules

## Qualification — all four must pass

1. **Live ads visible in the Meta Ad Library.** No live ads means no budget
   and no urgency. Skip, regardless of how much you like the product.
2. **Creative is stale or thin.** Same asset 4+ weeks, fewer than 4 active
   creatives, or static-only. If their creative is already strong and varied,
   they are not a prospect.
3. **Reachable decision maker.** Founder or a single marketing person,
   findable by name. Through an agency of record, the sale is 3x longer.
4. **Product is visually producible.** A physical product with existing
   photography.

## The verification rule

The observation in line one must be something **you personally looked at in
the last 48 hours.** Never write "your ads have been running since March"
unless you saw the date.

**DO NOT fabricate advertising observations. If current ad activity cannot be
verified, mark it UNVERIFIED.** An observation that turns out to be wrong
ends the conversation and earns a screenshot in someone's group chat.

## Never / Always

| Never | Always |
|---|---|
| "Hope you're doing well" | One observation they can verify you made |
| "I run an AI agency" — or lead with the tech at all | One ask, and make it free |
| A link in a first DM | An easy exit — "no reply needed if it's not useful" |
| A price in the opener | Stop at two follow-ups, then leave them 90 days |

## Follow-up ladder

- **Day 0** — the observation. One thing you saw, one free offer.
- **Day 4** — deliver value unasked. Send the work whether they replied or
  not. This converts better than the opener.
- **Day 11** — close out. "Going to stop bothering you." Then genuinely stop.

## Volume discipline

Below ~20 sends/day from a new domain you stay under filtering thresholds.
Do not buy cold-email tooling until 50/day is a real constraint. A burned
sending domain costs more than the tool saves.
