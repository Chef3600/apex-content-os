## Honest gaps — name these before a prospect does

- **No client work.** Every frame is spec. Say so first; it disarms the question.
- **One motion asset**, and it is low resolution. Short-form video is sold on a single 5-second clip today. This is the biggest hole in the library.
- **No people.** No hands, no service, no staff. Lifestyle and "real context" work cannot be demonstrated.
- **No multi-location or campaign set.** The "one shoot, six formats" claim is shown as a layout, not as a delivered campaign.
- **No hotel room, bar, or event interior** beyond the single hospitality frame.

**First paid job priority: get permission to use the work.** One real client set closes four of these five gaps at once.

# Entry Offers — structure only, no pricing

Pricing lives in `docs/offers.md` and every number there is a **test price** no
sale has confirmed. Do not quote from this file.

### 1 · Food Content Starter
- **Problem** — menu, site and delivery-app photos are phone photos, and the delivery app crops them to a square nobody framed for.
- **Deliverables** — one session, a fixed set of dishes, delivered in every format the channels need: square for delivery apps, vertical for social, wide for the site.
- **Who** — independent restaurants, chef-owned, new openings.
- **Why it matters** — the delivery-app thumbnail is often the first and only image a customer sees before ordering.
- **CTA** — "Would you be open to seeing a few content concepts we could build for your menu?"

### 2 · Restaurant Social Content
- **Problem** — posting cadence dies because there is nothing left to post.
- **Deliverables** — one production, cut into a month of scheduled social assets, stills and vertical video.
- **Who** — restaurants already posting but running dry.
- **Why it matters** — consistency beats occasional brilliance on every feed algorithm.
- **CTA** — same.

### 3 · Product Launch Content
- **Problem** — a launch date is fixed and there is no content library for it.
- **Deliverables** — hero, variants, vertical cut and lifestyle context, delivered before the launch date.
- **Who** — packaged food, beverage, consumer product brands with a dated launch.
- **Why it matters** — a launch without assets slips or ships weak.
- **CTA** — same.

### 4 · Hospitality Content Package
- **Problem** — property photos read like a real-estate listing, not a place worth visiting.
- **Deliverables** — environment, F&B and detail frames as one consistent set.
- **Who** — boutique hotels, venues, event spaces.
- **Why it matters** — the property's photos are the booking decision.
- **CTA** — same.

### 5 · Monthly Social Content *(the one that matters)*
- **Problem** — paid and organic creative fatigues every 3–6 weeks and in-house cannot keep up.
- **Deliverables** — a recurring monthly production feeding a fixed asset count across channels.
- **Who** — brands already running paid media. **Qualify via the Meta Ad Library: no observed ads, no prospect.**
- **Why it matters** — this is the only offer with recurring revenue, and the margin compounds. By month three you hold the brand kit, approved styles and shot library: same output, roughly half the hours.
- **CTA** — same.

### 6 · Brand Story Package
- **Problem** — the ads, the site and the feed look like three different companies.
- **Deliverables** — one visual system: direction, palette, framing rules, and a core asset set every channel draws from.
- **Who** — brands with content that exists but has drifted.
- **Why it matters** — inconsistency reads as smaller than you are.
- **CTA** — same.

**Never attached to any offer:** guaranteed results, performance claims, ROI
projections, invented clients, or unlimited revisions. Two revision rounds by
default; package limits govern where stated.
