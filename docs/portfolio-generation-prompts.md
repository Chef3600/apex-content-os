# Portfolio generation prompts — APEX AI CONTENT STUDIO

Thirteen assets. Food leads. Every one is **SPEC / PORTFOLIO CONCEPT** work for
invented brands — no real client product identity is reproduced anywhere here.

## Visual direction, applied to every prompt

Bright · premium · editorial · colorful · cinematic · modern · human ·
commercial. Daylight-forward, not dark-and-moody. The food supplies the
color; the frame stays restrained.

**This must not read as an AI-art gallery.** That means: real lens behavior,
plausible depth of field, imperfect natural surfaces, no hyper-saturation, no
glow, no symmetrical dead-center compositions, no impossible garnish physics.
Commercial photography, not illustration.

## Standing constraints on every generation

- No text, no logos, no packaging copy anywhere in frame. Brand identity is
  composited later from real vector files, never generated.
- No people's faces where a face would imply endorsement. Hands and
  over-shoulder working shots only.
- Nothing that misrepresents a real product. These are invented brands.
- Max 3 attempts per shot, then stop and redesign the shot.
