## Honest read

The big Las Vegas trade shows are the wrong move right now. They cost real
money, they consume days, and the exhibitors are mostly national brands with
agencies already retained.

**The free weekly local networking and the partnership angles are where a
studio with no clients actually gets its first three.** A web designer who
sends you every site build is worth more than a $2,000 expo badge.

One exception worth the effort: **White Label World Expo, Sep 23–24.** It is
two days away and the attendee list is private-label and CPG founders — the
exact profile that needs product photography and usually has none. Confirm
whether the expo hall is free with registration. If it is, go.
