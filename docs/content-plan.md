# SEO Plan

## Reality check first

The domain has **no history, no backlinks and no age**. Ranking for
"Las Vegas video production" against studios with a decade of citations is a
12–24 month project, not a 30-day one. Anyone promising faster is selling
something.

What is winnable in 90 days is the **long tail** — lower volume, far higher
intent, and almost no competition:

- "restaurant food photographer las vegas"
- "menu photography las vegas"
- "delivery app food photos las vegas"
- "packaged food product photography nevada"
- "beverage photography las vegas"
- "vertical video for restaurants las vegas"

The head terms ("las vegas video production", "las vegas content studio") are
the 12-month target, not the opening move.

> Keyword **volumes are not researched** — no web access this session. Treat
> the ordering above as intent-based reasoning, not measured data. Validate
> with Google Keyword Planner (free with an Ads account, no spend required)
> before committing to page builds.

## Pages to build — in priority order

Each is a real page at `apexhospitalitygrouplv.org`, not a blog post.

1. `/food-photography-las-vegas` — what a menu/delivery shoot includes, formats delivered, before/after pairs
2. `/restaurant-content-las-vegas` — the throughput argument, one shoot → six formats
3. `/product-photography-las-vegas` — packaged goods, label legibility, e-commerce sizes
4. `/beverage-photography-las-vegas` — backlight, condensation, pour work
5. `/hospitality-content-las-vegas` — rooms, service, property marketing

**Each page needs:** a unique title and meta description, one H1, real images
with descriptive alt text, the NAP block in the footer, a Start a Project CTA,
and `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD carrying the canonical NAP.

**Do not keyword stuff.** One primary phrase per page, used in the title, the
H1 and naturally in the body. The existing site already passes a strict
verifier — new pages must too, including the ASCII and American-English rules.

## Technical, already done

Canonical tags, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, Open Graph, Twitter cards and
`ProfessionalService` JSON-LD are live and verified. **Add each new page to
`sitemap.xml` as it ships** — the verifier does not yet catch a missing entry.

## Free tools worth connecting after launch

Google Search Console (the single most valuable free signal), Bing Webmaster
Tools, and Google Analytics or a lighter alternative. None cost money. Connect
Search Console the day the gate passes.
