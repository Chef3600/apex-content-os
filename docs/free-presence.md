## Order of operations — do not shuffle this

Two things gate the rest:

1. **The launch gate must pass** before any listing points the public at the
   site. A directory link to a site you have not loaded is a citation you will
   have to correct later, and corrections propagate slowly.
2. **Google Business Profile first**, alone, and let it settle. It is the only
   listing that meaningfully drives local discovery, and it is the one that
   scrapers copy. Get the NAP exactly right there before anything else
   replicates it.

Then work down Tier 1 → Tier 2. Do not bulk-submit. Ten accurate listings beat
sixty sloppy ones, and sloppy ones are worse than none.

## Rules — non-negotiable

- **No fake accounts. No fake reviews. No review solicitation that breaks a platform's terms.**
- No identical copy-paste across communities. That is what gets domains flagged.
- **No paid directories, no link farms, no "500 backlinks" services.** They are net-negative and hard to undo.
- Every listing that renders a portfolio must carry the **CONCEPT / SPEC WORK** label on the work.
- Leave a field blank rather than fill it with something untrue. See profile-kit §6.

## Tracking

Update this file's tables in place as each is created. The columns are the
record — `PROFILE CREATED`, `BACKLINK`, `DATE`, `STATUS`. A platform is not
"done" until the row says so with a date. Do not report a listing as created
until the account actually exists.
