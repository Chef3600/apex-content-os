# APEX CONTENT STUDIO — Master Profile Kit

**Every public listing, profile and bio copies from this file.** Do not retype
from memory. Search engines score local businesses partly on how consistently
name, address and phone (NAP) match across the web; a one-character drift
between two directories is a real cost, not a cosmetic one.

Canonical values are mirrored from `data/company.json`, which
`tools/verify-site.mjs` asserts against the live site. If you change identity,
change it there first.

## 3 · POSITIONING

**One line, used everywhere:**

> Content that drives business.

**Supporting line:**

> Commercial photography, video and visual content for brands that want to be seen.

**Three pillars — the public frame:**

1. **Commercial Content** — photography and video built for how the product sells.
2. **Social Media** — vertical, sound-off, made for the platform it runs on.
3. **Brand Storytelling** — a consistent visual system, not scattered one-offs.

**AI is not the pitch.** Production technology is internal infrastructure. It
may be mentioned factually in a process answer if a prospect asks how work is
produced. It never leads a headline, bio, service name, ad or opener. "AI
content studio" is a crowded, low-trust category; a working chef as the quality
gate is not.

Apex Content Studio is a commercial content studio in Las Vegas. We produce
photography and video for food, beverage, hospitality, packaged goods and
consumer product brands — the kind of work that has to carry a sale, not just

A single directed production feeds every channel a brand sells through. One

The studio is led by a working chef. Every frame of food is judged first on

### LONG — full profile (Clutch, Contra, agency directories, About pages)

```
Apex Content Studio is a commercial content studio based in Las Vegas, Nevada,
operating as Apex Hospitality Group LLC. We produce commercial photography and
video for brands that need their product to sell on a screen: food and
restaurant groups, beverage brands, hospitality, packaged goods and consumer
products.

WHAT WE PRODUCE

Commercial photography — food, packaging and product shot for advertising,
menus, e-commerce and press. Short-form video — vertical, built for the
platform it runs on, with the hook decided before the shoot. Product creative —
packaging heroes, texture macros and context frames that hold up at full zoom.
Ad creative — paid assets in the sizes platforms actually want, built as
variants from one production. Campaign production — direction, shot list,
production and delivery on a schedule. Content repurposing — existing footage
and stills recut and regraded into assets a brand can keep publishing.

HOW WE WORK

Advertising creative fatigues. A brand running paid media needs new assets
continuously, and most cannot produce them fast enough. What we sell is
production capacity and consistency — a directed shoot planned as a library
rather than a photograph, so one production feeds paid, organic, site and
email without the product looking like a different product in each place.

Direction comes first. Camera, light and composition are decided before
anything rolls, and the deliverable sizes are reserved in the frame during the
shoot rather than cropped in afterwards.

THE QUALITY GATE

The studio is led by a working chef. Food is judged as food before it is judged
as an image. That is the difference between a plate that photographs well and a
plate that is actually right.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO

We do not guarantee performance. We sell production capacity, not results. We
do not run media buying, manage ad accounts, or write offer and pricing
strategy. We do not present AI-generated people as real customers or produce
fabricated testimonials — under the FTC's 2024 rule that liability sits with
the advertiser, and we will not create that exposure for a client.

Apex Content Studio launches October 1, 2026, and is booking projects now.

Las Vegas · Henderson · North Las Vegas · Summerlin. Product and packaged goods
work ships anywhere.
```

## 6 · WHAT MAY NOT BE CLAIMED

Binding. These are the claims a directory form will tempt you into.

| Tempting field | The truth today |
|---|---|
| "Years in business" | Launching October 1, 2026. If a required field, use the LLC formation year and nothing more. |
| "Number of clients" | **Zero.** Leave blank. Never estimate. |
| "Notable clients" | None. Leave blank. |
| "Team size" | 1. Do not write "2-10" to look bigger. |
| "Portfolio" | Every asset is **concept / spec work** and must be labelled so wherever it appears. |
| Reviews / testimonials | None exist. Never write one, never ask a friend to write one. |
| "Starting price" | `docs/offers.md` prices are **test prices**, unconfirmed by any sale. Give a range only if required, and call it a starting range. |
| Results, ROI, lift | Never. We sell production capacity, not performance. |

If a platform requires a field that cannot be answered honestly, leave it
blank or do not use that platform. A blank field costs nothing. A fabricated
one costs the business.
