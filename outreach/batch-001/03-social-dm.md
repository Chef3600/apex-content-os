# SOCIAL DM — 0 prospects ready

**DM-READY: 0.**

Not one of the six has a verified official business social account. `social`
is `BLOCKED` on all six, because no handle was supplied and this runtime has
no outbound access to find one.

No DM has been drafted, because drafting a message for an account that has not
been verified to exist would be inventing a channel.

## What would change this

A verified official **business** account — Instagram, Facebook or LinkedIn —
with its source URL:

```
node tools/enrich.mjs set <id> social "@handle" --status=VERIFIED --source="<url>"
```

Three of the six (Slater's, Advanced Aesthetics, LuxeFactor) have **no email**,
so a verified Instagram would give each a proper second written channel rather
than leaving a contact form as the only route.

## Rules when DMs do become available

- Official **business** accounts only. Never a personal account unless that
  person has clearly published it for business contact.
- Shorter than the contact-form message. Two or three sentences.
- Same three-touch cap, same stop-on-request rule.
- Never message the same business on two platforms at once. One channel, one
  sequence.
