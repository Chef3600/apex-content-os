Process

How a production runs.

Five steps, the same on every job, whether the subject is a plated dish, an attorney, a treatment room or a truck.

01

Discovery

We understand the business, audience, offer, channels and deadline.

02

Creative Direction

We define the visual direction, shot list, locations, props, talent and deliverables.

03

Production

Photography and video are produced with the required lighting, composition and direction.

04

Post-Production

Editing, color, retouching, captions, resizing and final asset preparation.

05

Delivery

Files are delivered in the formats required for web, social, advertising and other channels.

Recurring Content

For businesses that need ongoing production, the same five steps run on a monthly calendar instead of a single booking, so the channels never run empty.
