Hospitality

We came from kitchens.

Food is the hardest thing to photograph well and the easiest thing to get subtly, fatally wrong.

A sear that reads as paint. Sauce that moves like plastic. Steam that behaves like smoke. Shrimp with the wrong number of tail segments. Most people producing food content cannot see those errors. Our founder has spent his career in professional kitchens and cannot stop seeing them.

How it works

Brief on Monday. Concepts by Friday.

  1. Consultation

    Twenty minutes. We look at what you are running now and tell you which angles you have not tested. You keep that whether or not you hire us.

  2. Intake

    One call covering product, audience, objective and the proof behind every claim you want made. If a claim has no proof, it does not go in an ad.

  3. Direction

    Five strategically distinct approaches, each with a hook, an objective and a call to action. You approve before anything is produced.

  4. Production

    Built from your existing photography and brand files. Your packaging and logo are used, never recreated. Nothing is invented about your product.

  5. Quality control

    Twenty-two categories, scored by a chef. Product truth, food realism, physics, continuity and compliance. One failure blocks the whole delivery.

  6. Delivery

    Every ratio, every platform, full commercial rights. One revision round on the entry project, two on the Sprint and three on the Campaign Build.

Questions

The things people actually ask.

How do you work so fast?

Modern production tools, directed shot by shot. Lens, lighting, camera move and framing are specified for every frame, the same way a director briefs a crew. It removes the scheduling, the location, the shipping and the crew day. It does not remove the direction, and it does not remove the judgment about whether the food looks right.

How is the work produced?

A mix of directed capture and modern digital production, chosen per shot for whatever serves the frame. Your product, packaging and logo always come from your real files. Claims come from your proofs. What gets built around them is the environment, the motion and the light. Anything that would misrepresent what you sell does not ship, and we will tell you exactly how any given asset was made if you ask.

Will it look real?

That is the right question, and the honest answer is that it depends entirely on the quality gate. Ours has twenty-two categories and food realism is scored by a chef. If a shot does not pass, it gets rebuilt or it gets cut. Ask us for a sample concept on your own product before you take our word for any of this.

What do you need from us?

Product photography, ideally several angles on a clean background, your logo as a vector file and twenty minutes on a call. If your photography is thin, we will tell you before we quote, not after.

Do we own what you make?

Yes. Full commercial usage rights on everything, all platforms, no expiration and no per-placement fees. That is included in the price rather than negotiated on top of it.

Can you guarantee results?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. We produce creative volume, variety and speed. Your results also depend on your offer, your pricing, your landing page and your media buying, none of which we touch. What we can tell you is that if creative production is your constraint, we remove that constraint.

What if we do not like it?

Revision rounds are included: one on the entry project, two on the Sprint and three on the Campaign Build. A revision changes an existing asset; a new concept is new work. We will say that up front rather than discover it with you halfway through.

Do you work outside food and drink?

Yes, product and brand work across categories. Food is where we are genuinely better than the alternative, so it is what we lead with. If you are outside the category, say what you need and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right studio.
